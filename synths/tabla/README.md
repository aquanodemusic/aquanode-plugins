# Tabla

![GUI](assets/GUI.png)

A JUCE-based tabla synthesizer plugin. It uses simple physical modelling techniques to get close to the unique tabla sound, but it is not a very close sound. The sound is relatively realistic though, even if it may not fully resemble a good tabla drum.

Each Tabla Set consists of a bass drum and a characteristic smaller drum, called bayan and dayan respectively.

You can tweak many parameters in the control section, such as drum size, hardness and brightness.
Different tabla sounds are mapped to the MIDI keyboard, you can however also click and drag the two drums with your mouse.
In this mouse click and drag mode, you can choose using the Slide Mode Toggle whether the drum should be hit continuously while you click and drag, or that the drum should change pitch while it still produces sound and you move the mouse around - moving farther away the pitch rises, moving into the center the pitch falls. This behaviour is also present in the original tabla drum, when you move the lower part of your palm over the drum after hitting it.
